# Exponential Product Strategy — One Page

**Status:** Adopted

**Category:** The open control plane for AI-native companies

**Operating method:** The Exponential Steering Loop

## Strategic choice

> **Exponential is the open control plane for AI-native companies.**

Exponential turns company goals into accountable weekly work for humans and AI agents. It gives a company one place to set direction, delegate work, surface decisions that require human judgment, and review what actually moved.

## Market thesis

AI is making execution abundant. Direction, accountability, and organizational learning are becoming the constraints.

Agent frameworks help companies build and run agents. Project tools record tasks and delivery. Agent builders supply AI labor. None of these is a stable, neutral management layer for a company whose work is performed by a changing mix of people and agents.

As execution accelerates, companies need a control plane that remains constant: shared goals, explicit commitments, governed delegation, visible evidence, and a recurring process for correcting course. That is the layer Exponential will own.

## Ideal customer and core job

Our initial customer is a founder-led, AI-native product company with roughly 5–50 people that already delegates meaningful work to multiple coding, research, support, or operations agents.

Their core job is:

> When my company delegates more work to AI agents, help me convert company goals into governed work and show me where human judgment is needed, so increased execution speed does not destroy alignment.

## The Exponential Steering Loop

Exponential provides an opinionated weekly management loop:

1. **Set goals** — establish the company direction and what matters now.
2. **Commit the week** — choose a small set of weekly priorities and accountable owners.
3. **Delegate work** — assign linked work to people or agents with clear authority and boundaries.
4. **Steer exceptions** — surface blockers, ambiguity, approvals, risk, and decisions in one human-attention queue.
5. **Review progress** — examine evidence, record what moved, and use what was learned to steer the next week.

The cadence is weekly; attention is continuous. Every commitment connects to a company goal. Every delegated action has a responsible human, even when an agent executes it. Every review improves the next loop.

## Product principles

- **Direction before activity.** Optimize for progress toward goals, not task volume or agent utilization.
- **Any agent, one company.** Codex, Claude Code, Agno, CrewAI, Hermes, and custom agents should work through stable interfaces without controlling the company model.
- **Agents are accountable principals.** Give agents identity, scoped access, revocable credentials, attributable activity, and auditable work.
- **Humans steer by exception.** Protect human attention by elevating the decisions, approvals, and failures that require judgment.
- **Open by design.** The core is inspectable, extensible, exportable, and self-hostable. Customers can use our cloud without surrendering control.
- **The loop compounds.** Goals, commitments, evidence, interventions, and reviews create a learning history that improves future planning and delegation.

## Positioning boundaries

- Agent platforms build and operate agents; **Exponential directs and governs the company work they perform**.
- Project trackers manage projects and tasks; **Exponential closes the loop from company goals to weekly commitments, delegated work, and review**.
- AI-workforce products sell agents; **Exponential makes a heterogeneous workforce accountable to one company direction**.

We will not compete on generic project-management breadth, the number of built-in agents, or an all-in-one feature catalogue. Meetings, projects, product planning, knowledge, CRM, and other modules support the Steering Loop; they do not define the acquisition story.

## Product and go-to-market focus

The immediate product priority is to make one complete loop undeniable: **goal → weekly commitment → delegated work → agent or human evidence → human decision → progress review**.

This requires excellent agent onboarding, goal-to-work lineage, a human-attention queue, agent activity and artifact history, approval controls, and a fast weekly review. Distribution should be open-source and agent-led: one-prompt installation and connection, first-class API/CLI/MCP documentation, public dogfooding, and design partners already running multiple agents.

The core self-hosted product remains complete. Managed cloud monetizes hosting, upgrades, backups, integrations, and AI convenience. Paid governance adds policy, observability, cost controls, audit retention, SSO, support, and—later—private or air-gapped deployment.

## Success measure

The north-star metric is **companies completing the Exponential Steering Loop in consecutive weeks**.

A completed loop includes a company goal, at least one weekly commitment, linked work delegated to a human or agent, attributable evidence or progress, and a human review. Activation means completing the first loop—and connecting the first agent—within 24 hours.

## Core message

> **Run your company with AI agents—without losing the plot.**
>
> Set company goals, delegate weekly priorities to people or agents, and see what actually moved.

**Strategy test:** if a product or marketing decision does not help an AI-native company turn goals into accountable weekly work, it is not a priority.
