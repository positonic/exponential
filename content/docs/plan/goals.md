Goals are the outcomes you are working toward. Each goal has an owner (the DRI), a period, a health, and the projects that execute it. Team and organisation workspaces use OKR vocabulary: a goal is an **Objective** and carries measurable [Key Results](/docs/plan/okrs).

![The Goals tab listing objectives with their owner, target period, projects and health](/doc-assets/goals.png)

## Where to find it

Open [Goals](/goals) — or **Align** → **Goals** in the sidebar. The page has two tabs: **Goals** (this page) and **OKRs**.

## How to create a goal

1. Click **+** at the top right of the **Goals** tab.
2. Type the goal in the **What's your goal?** field.
3. Pick a **DRI** — the directly responsible individual. This is required.
4. Open **More details** if you want to set a **Status** (Planned, Active, Completed, Archived), a **Parent goal** to nest under (up to 5 levels), **Why this goal?**, a **Due date**, a **Period** such as Q1 2026 or Annual 2026, a **Project**, or **Notes**. In a team workspace you can add key results here too.
5. Click **Create Goal**.

In a team workspace the same form says **objective** wherever this page says goal.

## How to find a goal

- Switch between **Active**, **Planned** and **Completed** with the pills above the list.
- Type in **Search** (⌘F) to filter by name.
- Click **Filter** to narrow by **Target** period, **Health** or **Owner**.
- Click **Mine** to show only goals you are the DRI on.
- Click **Timeline** to see the same goals laid out by period instead of as a list.
- Click the arrow beside a goal to expand its sub-goals and projects.

## How to work on a goal

Click a goal to open its page. It has **Overview**, **Activity** and **Projects** tabs, a **Features** tab when the Product Management plugin is on, and a settings tab (the gear).

- **Overview** shows sub-goals, the owner, the period and progress. Click the status badge to move the goal between **Active**, **Complete** and **On Hold**. Progress comes from key results; **Manual progress** lets you type a percentage, **Revert to auto (key results)** undoes that.
- **Activity** is where health lives. Choose **Update**, pick **On track**, **At risk** or **Off track**, and write what changed. The latest update is the goal's **Initiative Health** in the list (**No update** until then). **Comment** is for discussion that does not change health.
- **Projects** lists the projects delivering the goal. Link a project to a goal from the project form's **Link to Goals (Objectives)** field.

## How it connects

- **[OKRs](/docs/plan/okrs)** — the **OKRs** tab on the same page adds measurable key results to each objective and tracks them by quarter.
- **[Projects](/docs/do/projects)** — a project can serve several goals; the goal's **Projects** tab and **Active Projects** column show them.
- **[Weekly plan](/docs/reflect/weekly-plan)** — review your goals and check in on key results each week.
- **[Wheel of Life](/docs/plan/wheel-of-life)** — a life assessment that points at the areas worth a goal.

## Tips

- Give every goal a **Period**. Goals without one show **No target** and sit outside the timeline.

## FAQ

**Why does my workspace say "Objective" instead of "Goal"?**
Personal workspaces use plain language (goals). Team and organisation workspaces use OKR language (objectives and key results). They are the same object.

**Can I file a goal under a life domain?**
Not from the goal form. The **OKRs** tab can group objectives by life domain, and a project can be tagged with life domains from the project form.

**What happens when a goal is done?**
Set its status to **Complete** on the goal page; it moves from **Active** to **Completed**.
