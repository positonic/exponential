Plugins are optional feature sets you switch on per workspace. Turning one on adds its pages to the sidebar and its widgets to the dashboard; turning it off hides them without deleting any data.

![The Plugins tab in workspace settings](/doc-assets/plugins.png)

## Where to find it

[Workspace settings → **Plugins**](/go/settings/plugins): open the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar, choose the settings entry, then the **Plugins** tab.

## What's available

| Plugin | On by default | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| **OKRs (Objectives & Key Results)** v1.0.0 | Yes | Key results on goals and the **OKR Progress** dashboard widget. See [OKRs](/docs/plan/okrs). |
| **CRM (Contact Management)** v1.0.0 | Yes | [**Connect → CRM**](/go/crm) in the sidebar: contacts, organizations, interactions, lists and pipelines. See [Contacts](/docs/crm/contacts). |
| **Product Management** v0.1.0 | No | **Products** and **Metrics** under **Deliver** in the sidebar: products, features, tickets, research insights, cycles and retrospectives. See [Products, tickets, features & cycles](/docs/build/products). |

## How to enable or disable a plugin

1. Open workspace settings and click the **Plugins** tab.
2. Find the plugin's card and flip its switch. A **Plugin Enabled** or **Plugin Disabled** message confirms it.
3. The sidebar updates for everyone in the workspace.

## How it connects

- [Workspace settings](/docs/reference/workspace-settings#plugins) — the other tabs on the same page.
- [OKRs](/docs/plan/okrs) and [Contacts](/docs/crm/contacts) — what the two default plugins give you.
- [Goals](/docs/plan/goals) — with Product Management on, a goal gains a **Features** tab linking it to product work.

## FAQ

**Does disabling a plugin delete its data?**
No. Contacts, key results and product records stay where they are and reappear when you enable it again.

**Can I enable a plugin for just myself?**
No. Plugins are per workspace and apply to every member.

**Can I add my own plugin?**
Not yet — the three above are the only ones.
