Your settings are yours alone and apply in every workspace you belong to: how the sidebar is arranged, which theme you use, where notifications go, and which services and keys are connected to your account. Anything that changes a workspace for everyone lives in [Workspace settings](/docs/reference/workspace-settings).

![Settings → Navigation](/doc-assets/your-settings.png)

## Where to find it

Open your user menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Settings**, or go to [/settings](/settings). The tabs are grouped: **Account** (Profile, Notifications), **Workspace** (Navigation, Appearance), **Integrations** (Integrations, API keys, Agents) and **AI** (AI history, AI tools, AI assistant).

## Profile

Set your **Timezone** (calendar events and working hours are interpreted in it; the page tells you what your browser reports) and see the **Connected Accounts** you can sign in with: Discord, Google and Notion can each be linked to the same account.

## Notifications

Choose which notifications you get and where each one goes: push, email, Matrix, WhatsApp or Zulip. See [Notifications](/docs/collaborate/notifications).

## Navigation

The sidebar is yours to arrange:

- **Drag** sections and items to reorder them.
- **Click a section name** to rename it (for example, call "Deliver" "Work").
- **Toggle visibility** with the eye icon or the switch. Hidden items keep their data; nothing is deleted.
- **Reset** restores the default layout.

Items marked **plugin** (Products, Metrics) only appear when the workspace has that plugin on. Inbox, Today and Time are always shown.

Below the layout, **Home Screen** has three switches: **Daily Inspiring Quote**, **AI Suggested Focus** and **Browse All Quotes**.

## Appearance

**Color mode**: Light, Dark or System. **Dark theme style**: Navy or Slate.

## Integrations

Your personal connections to other services. See [Integrations](/docs/integrations).

## API keys

Tokens that let the iOS shortcut, the browser extension, the CLI and webhooks act as you. See [API tokens](/docs/developers/api-tokens).

## Agents

External agent software you run, each with its own identity and key. See [External agents](/docs/developers/external-agents).

## AI history

A log of your AI interactions across platforms and agents: totals, error rate, average response time, and a breakdown by platform and agent, filterable by workspace and project.

## AI tools

Two demonstrations: **AI Sales Demo** (AI-generated sales pages) and **AI Automation** (an overview of automation services).

## AI assistant

Create a personal assistant with its own **Name**, **Emoji**, **Personality & Soul**, optional **Instructions** and **About You / Your Team** context. This page is also where you connect **Telegram** and **Matrix** to chat with your assistant, choosing which agent answers by default (Zoe or a specialist). See [Zoe](/docs/zoe/assistant).

## How it connects

- **Workspace settings** — members, features, plugins and ceremonies are per workspace: [Workspace settings](/docs/reference/workspace-settings).
- **Notifications** — delivery preferences: [Notifications](/docs/collaborate/notifications).

## FAQ

**Will hiding a sidebar item hide it for my team?**
No. Navigation is personal; each member arranges their own.

**I hid something and cannot find it.**
Open **Settings → Navigation** and switch it back on, or click **Reset**.

**Where do I change my name or photo?**
They come from the account you signed in with. Link or switch providers under **Profile → Connected Accounts**.
