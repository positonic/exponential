A meeting is a conversation that happened: its transcript, an AI summary, the people who took part, and what came out of it. Meetings arrive from Fireflies automatically or you add them by hand, and each one can be turned into actions in a project and decisions in the Decision Log. A future booking is a scheduled meeting, made with **Schedule meeting**, and is a different thing.

![The Meetings page](/doc-assets/meetings.png)

## Where to find it

**Amplify → Meetings** in the sidebar, or [Meetings](/meetings) across all your workspaces. Each meeting opens on its own page.

## How to read the list

The header counts this week's meetings and the actions extracted from them. Tabs filter the list: **All**, **Mine** (meetings you own or attended), **1:1s**, **Customer**, **Internal**, **Archive**, and **Activity** (what Fireflies has sent recently). Search the box above the list for transcripts, people and topics, or narrow by ceremony.

Each card shows the date, duration, participants and the linked project, with two shortcuts: **Assign to project** (or the project's name once it has one) and **Show AI summary**.

## How to add a meeting by hand

Use this for meetings Fireflies did not record: pasted notes, a transcript from another tool, a call you took on paper.

1. Click **Add Meeting**.
2. Fill in **Title** and **Meeting Date**; a **Description** is optional.
3. Paste the **Transcript**, or write **Notes** (Markdown), or both.
4. Add **Participants** (teammates, CRM contacts, or new people by name and email), pick a **Project**, and link a **Ceremony** occurrence or **Features** if the meeting belongs to one.
5. Drop **Images** to attach screenshots or photos; they appear on the meeting's Screenshots tab.
6. Save. The meeting appears in the list and Zoe writes a summary when you open it.

## How to work with one meeting

![A meeting page: summary, actions, participants and links](/doc-assets/meeting-detail.png)

A meeting page has four tabs and a side rail.

- **Summary** — the AI summary (**Copy**, **Edit**, **Regenerate**), then two blocks: **Actions**, where **Create Actions** drafts actions from the transcript for you to review, and **Product features**, where **Ideate Features** proposes features for a product.
- **Transcript** — the full, speaker-attributed transcript with search; **Export transcript** in the rail downloads it.
- **Decisions** — the decisions and open questions logged from this meeting. **Extract decisions & questions** asks Zoe to draft them from the transcript; drafts are confirmed or rejected here and reach the Decision Log only once confirmed.
- **Screenshots** — images attached to the meeting.

The rail holds **Participants** (add or remove people; talk-time shows when the transcript has speakers), **Linked** (the project, with **Open project**; **Link to an occurrence** for a ceremony; **Link to features**), **Details**, and the actions **Share with team** (copies the link), **Export transcript** and **Archive meeting**.

## How to turn a meeting into actions

Nothing is created without your review.

1. Assign the meeting to a project (from the card's **Assign to project** or the rail's **Linked** block).
2. On the **Summary** tab click **Create Actions**. Zoe drafts actions from the transcript.
3. Edit or delete each draft, then confirm the ones you want. They are added to the project and show up on [Today](/today) or in the project's board like any other [action](/docs/do/actions).

If the meeting already has actions, **Create Actions** tells you so instead of drafting duplicates.

## How to log the decisions

1. Open the **Decisions** tab and click **Extract decisions & questions**.
2. Review the drafts with Zoe: confirm the real decisions, reject the rest. An open question is a decision with status Open.
3. Confirmed decisions appear in **Align → Decisions** with the quote from the transcript they came from.

You can also log a decision by hand from the same tab.

## How to archive or delete

**Archive meeting** in the rail moves a meeting to the **Archive** tab, where it can be restored. Deleting is permanent and available from the list.

## FAQ

**The summary is empty or wrong.**
Click **Regenerate** on the Summary tab, or **Edit** to write your own.

**Create Actions found nothing.**
The transcript has no action-like statements, or it is too short. Add notes with the follow-ups and try again.

**Who can see a meeting?**
Members of its workspace; a meeting in a restricted project follows that project's access list. **Share with team** copies the link for people who already have access.

**Can I schedule a meeting from here?**
**Schedule meeting** books a future meeting with workspace members and sends calendar invites; it does not create a recorded meeting. A guide for scheduling and calendars is coming.
