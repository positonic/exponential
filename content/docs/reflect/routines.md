Two guided pages bookend the day. The Startup routine (**Start Your Day With Intention**) collects what you will do for your body, what you are grateful for and a journal entry. The Wind-down routine (**End Your Day With Reflection**) asks what went well, how your energy was, what you learned and how you handled challenges. Everything you save lands on today's record under [Days](/days).

![The Startup routine with its Converge, Diverge and Synthesize sections](/doc-assets/startup-routine.png)

## Where to find it

Open [/startup-routine](/startup-routine) and [/wind-down](/wind-down). Neither page is in the sidebar or the user menu, so bookmark them. Opening today's day from **Days** in the user menu shows the same startup routine.

## How to run the startup routine

The page has three sections. Each field has its own save button, and **Save All Progress** at the bottom saves everything at once.

1. **Converge** — under **Exercise**, write what you will do to nourish your body today and click **Save Exercise** (or press ⌘↵).
2. **Diverge** — under **Gratitude**, write what you are grateful for and click **Save Gratitude**. Under **Journal**, write a free-form entry and click **Save Journal**. The editor is the same one as the [Journal](/docs/reflect/journal) page, with the same formatting toolbar.
3. **Synthesize** — the wind-down sections, embedded so you can fill them in from the same page in the evening.
4. Click **Save All Progress**. **Last saved** updates when the save completes.

### Optional Converge sections

Click the gear icon below the sections to show more prompts in Converge: **Show Mindset Section**, **Show Consider Section**, **Show Not-to-Do List** and **Show Important Questions**. Mindset, Consider and Important Questions are fixed checklists you tick as you go. The **Not-to-Do List** takes your own items: type one, click **Add**, and tick it later when you avoided it. These toggles are not remembered between visits.

## How to run the wind-down routine

1. Open [/wind-down](/wind-down).
2. **What went well today?** — write, then click **Save What Went Well**.
3. **Energy Reflection** — answer "How was your energy level today?" and click **Save Energy Reflection**.
4. **Learning and Growth** — answer "What did I learn today?" and click **Save Learning & Growth**.
5. **Reflection on Challenges** — four prompts, each with its own button: how you handled challenges (**Save Challenges Reflection**), where you fell from grace (**Save Mistakes**), what caused it (**Save Causes**) and what you will do next time (**Save Solutions**).
6. **Daily Habits** — tick **Did you meditate today?** and **Did you log your food today?**, then click **Save Progress**.

## How it connects

- **[Journal](/docs/reflect/journal)** — the Journal page is the standalone editor; **Days** lists every day you saved a routine on.
- **[Habits](/docs/reflect/habits)** — for habits you want to track with streaks, rather than the two fixed checkboxes at the end of the wind-down.
- **[Weekly plan](/docs/reflect/weekly-plan)** — a week of routine entries is the raw material for the weekly review.

## FAQ

**Where do my entries go?**
To today's record on your account, so they follow you between devices. The exception is the two **Daily Habits** checkboxes at the end of the wind-down, which are kept in this browser only.

**Can I fill in a routine for a past day?**
Not yet. Both pages always write to today.

**Can I change the prompts?**
No. You can show or hide the optional Converge sections, but the questions themselves are fixed.

**Why does "Creating day record…" show at the bottom?**
The first time you open the startup routine on a given day, Exponential creates that day's record. Saving is enabled once it exists.
