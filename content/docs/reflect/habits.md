Habits are recurring practices you want to keep up: daily, a few times a week, weekly, monthly, or on days you choose. The Habits page shows what is due today, lets you tick each one off, and keeps streaks and a 90-day activity calendar. A habit can be linked to a [Goal](/docs/plan/goals) so the daily practice sits next to what it serves.

![The Habits page with stats, the Weekly Plan card, today's habits and the habit list](/doc-assets/habits.png)

## Where to find it

Open [/habits](/habits). The page is not in the sidebar or the user menu, so bookmark it. Habits due today also appear in the **Habits** card on your home dashboard.

## How to create a habit

1. Click **Add Habit** (top right).
2. Type the habit in the title field ("What habit do you want to build?") and, optionally, a **Description**.
3. Pick a **Frequency**: **Daily**, **3x per week**, **Weekly**, **Bi-weekly**, **Monthly** or **Custom days**. For **3x per week** and **Custom days**, choose the **Days of the week**.
4. Optionally set a **Reminder time**, a **Start date**, an **End date** and **Link to goal**.
5. Click **Create Habit**.

## How to tick off a habit

1. Under **Today's Habits**, tick the checkbox next to the habit. The counter (**2 / 3 completed**) updates.
2. Tick it again to undo.

Which habits are due today depends on the frequency: **Daily** every day; **Weekly** on Sundays; **Bi-weekly** every other Sunday from the start date; **Monthly** on the 1st; **3x per week** and **Custom days** on the days you picked. A habit before its start date or after its end date is not listed.

## How to read streaks and the calendar

- The three cards at the top show **Active Habits**, **Completions (90 days)** and **Best Current Streak**.
- The flame badge on a habit is its current streak: consecutive days with a completion, counting back from today or yesterday, whatever the habit's frequency.
- **All Activity (Last 90 Days)** appears once you have any completions. Each habit card also shows its **Last 30 days**.
- Switch the list between **Active**, **Inactive** and **All**.

## How to edit or delete a habit

1. Open the **⋯** menu on the habit card.
2. Click **Edit**, change the fields and click **Update Habit** — or click **Delete** and confirm. Deleting removes the habit and its history.

## How it connects

- **[Goals](/docs/plan/goals)** — a habit linked to a goal shows the goal's title on its card.
- **[Weekly plan](/docs/reflect/weekly-plan)** — the **Weekly Rituals** card at the top of the page shows whether this week's plan is done, your streak in weeks, and a **Start** or **View** button.
- **[Routines](/docs/reflect/routines)** — the wind-down routine ends with two fixed daily checkboxes; anything you want a streak for belongs here instead.

## FAQ

**Does the reminder time send a notification?**
Not yet. It is saved with the habit, but nothing sends a reminder.

**Can I pause a habit without deleting it?**
Not from the app yet. Set an **End date** instead: the habit stops appearing in today's list after that date and keeps its history.

**Can I add a note, duration or rating to a completion?**
Not yet. Ticking the checkbox records the completion for today only.

**Why does my weekly habit only show up on Sundays?**
That is how **Weekly** and **Bi-weekly** are scheduled today. If you want it on a different day, use **Custom days** and pick the day.
