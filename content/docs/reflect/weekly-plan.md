The Weekly plan is a guided review you run once a week. It walks across every workspace you belong to in four phases: pick which workspaces are in focus and give each a theme, check in on the quarter's key results, sort projects into **Top focus**, **Active** and **Backlog**, and finish with a summary that links into each workspace's own weekly plan. Each finished week adds to your streak, and past weeks are kept in a history.

![The Weekly plan intro with the quarter at a glance and the Start portfolio review button](/doc-assets/weekly-plan.png)

## Where to find it

Open [/weekly-plan](/weekly-plan). It is not in the sidebar or the user menu; the **Rituals** cards on your home dashboard link to it as **Portfolio Review**, and the [Habits](/docs/reflect/habits) page has a **Weekly Plan** card. Past weeks are at [/weekly-plan/history](/weekly-plan/history).

## How to run the review

1. The intro shows the week, your streak, **KR confidence** and **Quarter pace** for the current quarter, and how many workspaces, objectives, projects and due actions you have. Click **Start portfolio review**.
2. **Phase 1 · Triage workspaces** — click **Bring into focus** on each workspace you will actually work in this week and type a **Theme** ("What does winning this week look like?"). Click **Mark dormant** to drop one. Dormant workspaces stay visible but do not surface in the later phases. Click **Continue**.
3. **Phase 2 · Re-rank objectives** — pick a workspace tab and a quarter. Expand an objective to see its key results. Click **Focus on** to make a key result this week's focus, and click its current value (for example **3/10**) to type a new one, which logs a check-in. **Create goal** and **Add key result** are there if something is missing. Click **Continue**.
4. **Phase 3 · Prioritize projects** — every project from your in-focus workspaces sits in one of three tiers: **Top focus** (at most 5), **Active** and **Backlog**. Use the **Top**, **Active** and **Backlog** buttons on a row to move it; this changes the project's priority everywhere, not just here. Filter with the workspace chips. If projects linked to a focused key result are not in Top focus, a banner offers **Promote all**. Click **Finish review**.
5. **Phase 4 · Drill in** — the summary reads "Portfolio set for the week." with your streak and a **Drill into a workspace** list. Each entry opens that workspace's own weekly plan in a new tab. Click **Done — back to home** when you are finished.

Close the review with the **X** at the top right at any time. Focus choices, themes and check-ins save as you make them, and reopening the page resumes where you stopped.

## How to review one workspace

Each workspace has its own weekly plan, reached from the drill-in list, the **Weekly Plan** ritual card on the home dashboard, or the card on the Habits page. It goes through the workspace's active projects one at a time, least healthy first:

1. Choose **Quick Sweep** or **Full Review** and a timer (**10 min**, **15 min**, **25 min** or **No limit**).
2. For each project, update **Status** and **Priority**, read the derived **Health**, capture a next action, update linked key results, and note what changed since last week.
3. Finish to record the week and extend that workspace's streak.

## How to share a team check-in

Each workspace also has a **Weekly Team Check-in** page, linked from the **Rituals** cards on the home dashboard. It lists the workspace's active projects with **Status**, **Priority** and a read-only column of their actions, and it is what you share with a team.

1. Click **Sharing Settings** and switch on each **organization team** that may see your plan, or click **Enable All Teams**. Only teams marked **Organization Team** in their [team settings](/docs/collaborate/teams) appear here.
2. Back on the check-in page, **Weekly Plan Sharing** shows **Shared** with a copyable link per team. Team members find it on the team's **Weekly Plans** tab.
3. To post it to Slack, click **Send to Slack**, pick the integration and channel, and confirm. The button is disabled until the workspace has a [Slack integration](/docs/integrations/slack).

## How it connects

- **[Goals](/docs/plan/goals)** and **[OKRs](/docs/plan/okrs)** — Phase 2 reads the quarter's objectives and writes key-result check-ins.
- **[Projects](/docs/do/projects)** — Phase 3 sets project priority; the per-workspace plan sets status too.
- **[Teams](/docs/collaborate/teams)** — organization teams receive shared check-ins.
- **[Habits](/docs/reflect/habits)** — the weekly plan counts as a weekly ritual with its own streak.
- **[Journal](/docs/reflect/journal)** — reread the week's days before you set next week's theme.

## FAQ

**Do I have to review every workspace?**
No. Only the workspaces you bring into focus appear in phases 2 and 3.

**What counts toward the streak?**
Clicking **Finish review** in a given week. The per-workspace plan keeps a separate streak.

**Can I change something after finishing?**
Yes. Reopen [/weekly-plan](/weekly-plan) and use the phase bar at the top to jump back to any phase.

**Where did weekly commitments go?**
The check-in no longer has its own commitments list. Set focus key results in Phase 2 and Top-focus projects in Phase 3 instead.
