Exponential runs as a web app you can install on iPhone or Android. Installed, it opens full-screen from your home screen with a bottom bar for **Inbox**, **Today**, **Calendar** and **Plan**, and it can send you push notifications.

## Where to find it

There is no App Store or Play Store listing. Open Exponential in your phone's browser and install it from the browser menu, as below.

## How to install on iPhone

1. Open Exponential in **Safari** (other iOS browsers cannot install web apps).
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

## How to install on Android

1. Open Exponential in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu.
3. Tap **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**) and confirm.

## How to turn on push notifications

1. In the installed app, go to [Settings → Notifications](/settings/notifications).
2. Under **Push Notifications**, tap **Enable Notifications** and accept the permission prompt.
3. To stop them, come back and tap **Disable**.

On iPhone, push only works from the installed home-screen app, not from a Safari tab. Which notifications reach you is set on the same page — see [Notifications](/docs/collaborate/notifications#how-to-enable-push).

## How it connects

- [Inbox and Today](/docs/do/inbox-and-today) — the two bottom-bar views you will use most on the phone.
- [Notifications](/docs/collaborate/notifications) — choose which events arrive as push.
- [iOS shortcut](/docs/capture/ios-shortcut) — capture by voice with Siri without opening the app at all.
- [WhatsApp](/docs/zoe/whatsapp) — talk to Zoe from your messaging app instead.

## FAQ

**Does it work offline?**
No. The installed app needs a connection; nothing is cached for offline reading.

**Is it different from the website?**
Same app, same account. Installing gives you a home-screen icon, a full-screen window and push notifications.

**I see "Push notifications are not supported on this device".**
Your browser cannot receive web push. On iPhone, install the app to your home screen first and open it from there.

**Notifications are blocked.**
Allow notifications for Exponential in your phone's settings, then tap **Enable Notifications** again.
