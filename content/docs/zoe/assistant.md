Zoe is the assistant built into Exponential. Ask her what to focus on today, get a project broken down into actions, or think something through — and tag a specialist agent with @ when you need one. Zoe works with you in a conversation and acts as you, with your confirmation.

![The Agents page with a new Zoe chat](/doc-assets/agent.png)

## Where to find it

Zoe is available three ways:

- [**Amplify → Agents**](/go/agent) in the sidebar ([/agent](/agent)) — the full chat page, with your chat history and the specialist agents.
- The **Ask Zoe · ⌘J** button in the bottom-right corner of every page, which opens the Zoe drawer beside whatever you are working on. Press **⌘J** to toggle it and **Esc** to close it.
- The command palette (**⌘K**) — pick the **Ask Zoe** mode and the drawer opens.

You can also message Zoe from your phone — see [Chat gateways](/docs/zoe/chat-gateways).

## How to start a chat

1. Open [**Amplify → Agents**](/go/agent) in the sidebar.
2. Click **New chat**. Zoe greets you and asks what's up.
3. Type in the **Ask anything** box and press Enter, or click the microphone to speak instead of typing.
4. Earlier conversations are listed under **Your Chats**. Use **Search chats...** to find one and click it to pick up where you left off.

## How to use a specialist agent

Specialist agents handle narrower jobs than Zoe. They are listed under **Specialist Agents** in the left column of the Agents page.

1. Start your message with `@` and the agent's name — a list appears as you type.
2. Write the rest of the message and send it. That message goes to the specialist instead of Zoe.
3. Messages without an @mention go to Zoe. You can also switch with the **Active agent** menu at the top of the chat.

## How to use the Zoe drawer

The drawer keeps Zoe alongside the page you are on, so you can ask about what is in front of you.

1. Click **Ask Zoe · ⌘J** (bottom right) or press **⌘J**.
2. Type in the box at the bottom. The header says whether you are in a **New thread** or a **Resumed thread**.
3. Click **History** to reopen one of your **Recent Conversations**, or **New thread** to start fresh.
4. Use the width toggle to make the drawer wider or narrower, or **Maximise** to open it as a full modal. Click the agent name in the header to switch between your assistant and a specialist.

## How to give Zoe a name and personality

1. Open [Settings → AI assistant](/settings/assistant).
2. Enter a **Name** and pick an **Emoji** — this is how your assistant introduces itself everywhere, including the drawer and WhatsApp.
3. Describe the personality you want in the text box and save.

## How it connects

- **Keyboard shortcuts** — ⌘J for the drawer and the palette's Ask Zoe mode: [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/reference/keyboard-shortcuts).

- [Knowledge base](/docs/zoe/knowledge-base) — pin a resource there and it is included in every chat, so Zoe answers from your own documents and meetings.
- [Chat gateways](/docs/zoe/chat-gateways) — message Zoe from your phone; the **Connect WhatsApp** button on the Agents page starts the setup.
- [External agents](/docs/developers/external-agents) — for software that should act under its own name rather than yours.
- [Settings → AI history](/settings/ai-history) — every message you have exchanged with Zoe, searchable and filterable by platform.

## Tips

- Rate a reply with the stars under it. A short note on what would have made it better improves future answers.
- Ask for the outcome, not the mechanics: "plan my afternoon around the launch" gets a better answer than "list my actions".

## FAQ

**Is Zoe the same as Paddy?**
Paddy no longer exists. There is one assistant, Zoe, plus specialist agents you can @mention and [external agents](/docs/developers/external-agents) you connect yourself.

**Does Zoe do things without asking?**
No. Zoe acts as you, inside the conversation, and confirms before creating or changing things. Software that should work on its own, under its own name, is an external agent.

**Where do my chats live?**
Under **Your Chats** on the Agents page and under **History** in the drawer. The full log is in [Settings → AI history](/settings/ai-history).

**Can Zoe read my meetings and documents?**
What is in your [knowledge base](/docs/zoe/knowledge-base). Pin a resource there to have it in every chat.
