Inbox and Today are the two views you open every day. **Inbox** holds every action that has no date and no project yet, so nothing you capture gets lost. **Today** shows what is due today and what is overdue, beside your calendar, with **Tomorrow** and **Upcoming** a tab away.

![The Inbox listing unassigned actions with the tag filter and Bulk edit control](/doc-assets/inbox.png)

## Where to find it

Open [Inbox](/inbox) or [Today](/today) — they are the first two items in the sidebar, above **Time**. The Inbox count in the sidebar is the number of actions waiting there. [Upcoming](/upcoming) is a plain list of what is scheduled after tomorrow; the **Upcoming** tab on Today shows the same actions grouped by day.

## How to clear the Inbox

An action lands in the Inbox when it has no due date, no scheduled time and no project. It leaves as soon as you give it any of those.

1. Click an action to open it.
2. Set a due date, or pick a **Project**, or both.
3. Click **Save changes**. The action moves to Today, Upcoming or its project.

To handle several at once, click **Bulk edit**, tick the actions, then **Reschedule** them to a date, **Move** them to a project, or **Delete** them. Use **Filter by tags…** to work through one tag at a time. When the Inbox is empty you get a small celebration.

## How to work from Today

![Today with the overdue section open, the calendar rail and the Add task button](/doc-assets/today.png)

1. Open the **Overdue** section at the top. Each row shows how long ago it was due. Click **Reschedule all → Today** to pull everything into today, or click the calendar icon on a row to reschedule just that one.
2. Work down the list. Tick the circle to complete an action; it stays visible as done for the rest of the day.
3. Click **Add task** at the bottom to capture something new with today's date.
4. Click **Plan** at the top to open the daily plan, and the score ring for your **Daily Productivity Score**.

The rail on the right shows today's calendar events from a connected calendar (see [Integrations](/docs/integrations)). Switch to **Tomorrow** or **Upcoming** with the tabs at the top right; **Upcoming** groups actions by day. **Filter by tags…** and **Bulk edit** work here too, and **Exit bulk** returns to the normal list.

## How it connects

- **Calendar** — the events in Today's rail come from your connected calendars and feeds: [Calendar & scheduling](/docs/meet/calendar).

- **[Actions](/docs/do/actions)** — everything in these views is an action; the workspace board under **Deliver** → **Actions** shows the same actions by status.
- **[Projects](/docs/do/projects)** — assigning a project is the usual way out of the Inbox; each project's **Tasks** tab shows only its own actions.
- **[Routines](/docs/reflect/routines)** — the startup routine and wind-down are built around clearing the Inbox and reviewing Today.
- **[Weekly plan](/docs/reflect/weekly-plan)** — pick the week's actions from Upcoming when you plan.

## Tips

- Capture first, sort later. Anything you add without a date or project goes to the Inbox, so **Create Action** (⌘N) from anywhere is safe.
- Overdue actions that were created together are grouped, so you can reschedule a batch that came from one meeting in one click.

## FAQ

**Why is an action with a due date not in my Inbox?**
The Inbox only holds actions with no date and no project. A dated action is on Today or Upcoming; a project action is on its project's **Tasks** tab.

**Is there a "this week" or "this month" view?**
Not as separate tabs. **Upcoming** groups everything after tomorrow by day.

**Can I reschedule overdue actions to a date other than today?**
Yes. Use the calendar icon on a row, or **Bulk edit** → **Reschedule** to pick any date.
