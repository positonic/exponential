The daily plan is a short routine you run once a day, for today or for tomorrow: pick the work, estimate how long each piece takes, fill the rest of the day from your existing actions and calendar, put it in priority order, lay it on a time grid, and write it up. The result is what [Today](/today) shows as planned, and the daily summary and the home page banner nudge you until it is done.

![The daily plan wizard](/doc-assets/daily-plan.png)

## Where to find it

[Daily plan](/daily-plan), the **Plan** button at the top right of [Today](/today), and the **Plan** tab of the bottom bar on a phone. The **Daily Plan Banner** feature in workspace settings shows a reminder on the home page while the plan is unfinished.

## How to plan a day

The wizard has a welcome step and six numbered steps (Fill task list and Prioritize share a screen); you can go back at any point.

1. **Which day?** Choose **Today** or **Tomorrow**. In the afternoon it suggests planning tomorrow instead. Click **Plan today** (or tomorrow).
2. **Add a task.** Name one thing you want to work on and click **Add**, or pick from the panel of existing actions that are overdue or due today. What you pick appears under **Planned for today**.
3. **Estimate timing.** Set the time you plan to spend on each item. The total is compared with your work hours, and the plan says **Over capacity** when it does not fit.
4. **Fill task list** and **Prioritize** (one screen). Create more actions, or pull in work from your existing tools: import calendar events for the day, or add actions from Notion. Drag the list into the order you will work it, and drag anything that will not fit to a later day to defer it. Press **A** to open the add box, **Esc** to close it.
5. **Schedule.** Drag items onto the day's time grid to time-block them between your work hours (set under [Settings → Profile](/settings/profile)), or skip this step. **Get Suggestions** asks for **AI Scheduling Suggestions** for anything still unscheduled and lets you apply them in one go.
6. **Document.** A summary card of the plan plus a place for **Obstacles in my way**; this is what you share or come back to. Finish, and Today reflects the plan.

A plan belongs to its date. Reopen the wizard the same day to adjust it; the next day starts a new one.

## FAQ

**Do I have to do all six steps?**
No. Adding one action and finishing is a valid plan; the later steps are there when the day needs shaping.

**Can Zoe plan the day for me?**
Ask her ("plan my day") from the Zoe drawer; she uses the same actions and calendar. The wizard is the hands-on version.

**Where did my plan go?**
Plans are per date. Open [Daily plan](/daily-plan) and choose the same day; yesterday's plan is not shown, but the actions it scheduled keep their times.
