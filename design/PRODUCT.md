# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Small, AI-first product teams - founders and their first collaborators - who coordinate goals, projects, and execution across humans and AI agents. The primary users are experts fluent in tools like Linear and Notion who live in this app daily; agents are first-class actors on the same surfaces (they file tickets, comment, and run automations). The team building Exponential dogfoods it as its own tracker, so the builders are also the heaviest users.

## Product Purpose

Exponential is the operating system for AI-native organizations: goals cascade into projects, projects into actions and tickets, and AI agents handle execution alongside people. It replaces the scatter of project tracker + CRM + meeting notes + OKR sheet with one connected workspace. Success is a team that steers by goals while the mechanical work coordinates itself.

## Positioning

Humans set direction; the system and its agents handle the mechanics. Every screen should make direction obvious and mechanics disappear.

## Brand Personality

Focused, quiet, precise. The tool disappears into the task: dense but calm surfaces, color only where it carries meaning (status, priority, identity), copy that is literal and short. Confidence through restraint - in the Linear/Raycast school, never showy.

## Anti-references

- Jira and enterprise clutter: toolbar soup, config-everything, chrome competing with content.
- The generic AI-startup look: purple-blue gradients, glassmorphism, sparkles, landing-page effects inside product UI.
- Bootstrap/admin-template blandness: default cards and shadows, an interchangeable dashboard with no committed choices.
- Playful consumer softness: pastel-everything, mascots, celebration animations in serious workflows.
- The stuffed, pressed everything-app: cramming every capability onto every screen until nothing has room to breathe.

## Design Principles

- The tool disappears into the task: familiarity is a feature; never invent an affordance where a standard one exists.
- One paradigm per job: the same activity feed, the same pill, the same drawer everywhere - changes in one place carry everywhere.
- Direction over mechanics: primary surfaces show goals, status, and what needs deciding; automation details stay behind progressive disclosure.
- Instant feedback: interactions are optimistic; the user never waits on a round trip to see their own change.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Best effort, no formal WCAG commitment yet: no obvious contrast failures, visible focus states on interactive elements, and keyboard-reachable flows where they come naturally with the component library.
